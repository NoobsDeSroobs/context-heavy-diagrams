# Code of conduct

This applies to everything around the project: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where people talk about it here on GitHub. It is the author's personal project (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)), so the author decides what is acceptable, and the aim is simple: people can report a bug, ask a question or suggest an idea while maintaining a civil discourse all around.

## What is expected

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people who do not share your first language or your experience.
- Take feedback on your own work as it is meant, and give it in a way that helps.
- Assume good faith until there is a reason not to.

## What is not accepted

- Discourtesy.
- Sensitive skin.
- Politics.
- Religion.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, and anything else that is plainly not about the project.

## What happens

The author may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, lock a thread, or block someone from the repository, with or without a warning, depending on how serious it is. Decisions are the author's, but civil argumentation against the decision is welcome.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment or issue, or write to the author through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are read by the author alone and treated privately. Security problems go to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not here.
