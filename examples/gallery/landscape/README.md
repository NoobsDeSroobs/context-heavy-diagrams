# Landscape

- **`meridian-rail-landscape.chd`**: Meridian Rail's as-is IT landscape, about 70 services across 10 business domains, each tagged for GDPR data sensitivity, NIS2 scope, owning team, business domain and lifecycle state, with the integration edges between them. The GDPR and NIS2 views are highlights over this one diagram (toggle them at the bottom left), the one place in the gallery where a single base diagram is reused rather than redrawn ([ADR 0028](../../../docs/decisions/0028-example-gallery-shared-landscape-and-domain-folders.md)).
- **`meridian-rail-landscape-layered.chd`**: the same estate as a drill-down, one shape per business domain. Ctrl/Cmd-click **Ticketing & Booking** to open `ticketing-and-booking.chd`, its seven services, and from there Booking Session Manager's target-state AWS deployment, `aws-deployment.chd`. Only this domain is drawn.
