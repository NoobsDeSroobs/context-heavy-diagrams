# National Rail Interop Network

A shared, multi-operator system — not owned by Meridian Rail — that lets tickets issued by one national or regional rail operator be validated and honored by others, for journeys that cross operator boundaries.

## Role

Validates that a ticket issued by Meridian Rail is recognized as legitimate by whichever operator actually carries the passenger for a given leg of a longer, multi-operator journey, and vice versa.

## Why it matters here

This is part of why NIS2 (critical-infrastructure/essential-service regulation) applies to Meridian Rail at all — participation in shared national transport infrastructure, not just running its own network. See the NIS2 highlight overlay (epic 0022 entry #3, not yet built) once it exists.
