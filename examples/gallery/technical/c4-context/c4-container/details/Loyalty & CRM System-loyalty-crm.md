# Loyalty & CRM System

An existing Meridian Rail system, predating this modernization program. Not being replaced — the Ticketing & Booking Platform integrates with it rather than absorbing its responsibilities.

## Role

Holds passenger profiles, contact preferences, and the loyalty points program (points earned per journey, redeemable for fare discounts).

## Why it matters here

The "syncs customer profile & loyalty points with" relationship is why passenger personal data (profile, journey history) flows beyond the ticketing platform itself — relevant to the GDPR highlight overlay (epic 0022 entry #2, not yet built).
