# Station Staff

Meridian Rail employees working station ticket counters and platforms. Assist passengers who can't or don't want to use the self-service app: issuing walk-up tickets, handling group bookings, processing on-the-spot refunds, and assisting travelers with accessibility needs.

Access the platform through a staff-facing interface — not modeled separately at this level of detail, see the Container diagram once it exists — distinct from the passenger-facing app, even though both ultimately talk to the same Ticketing & Booking Platform shown here.
