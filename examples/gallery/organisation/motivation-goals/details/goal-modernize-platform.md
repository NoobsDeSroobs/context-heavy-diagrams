# Goal: Modernize Ticketing Platform

Replace the legacy mainframe-based reservation system with the new Ticketing & Booking Platform (see the technical C4 diagrams). Greenfield project to develop a platform designed around the modern booking experience passengers now expect, and free of the key-person and vendor-support risk the current system carries.
Important to note that it is not a like-for-like rebuild.

This is the goal the majority of the task force's day-to-day work sits under. The Context/Container/Component diagrams define what "modernize" actually means in concrete terms for Meridian Rail.
