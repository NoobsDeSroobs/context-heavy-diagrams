# Driver: Legacy System Risk

The current reservation platform runs on a mainframe system that predates most of the current engineering team. Three concrete risks:

- **Key-person risk** — a shrinking number of people who actually understand the system well enough to safely change it.
- **Vendor support risk** — the platform's underlying stack is approaching or has already passed end-of-support from its original vendor.
- **Change risk** — every change to the current estate is slow and high-stakes specifically *because* of the first two risks, which compounds the problem over time rather than staying static.

This is the driver most directly responsible for the shape of the technical work.
