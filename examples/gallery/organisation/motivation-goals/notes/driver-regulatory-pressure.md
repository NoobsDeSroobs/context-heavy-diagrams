# Driver: Regulatory Pressure

Two regulatory regimes apply to Meridian Rail that the current estate wasn't designed with in mind:

- **GDPR** — passenger personal data (names, journey history, payment metadata) flows through several systems with no consolidated view of what's collected, why, or for how long. No assessment of lawful bases for processing and storing our data.
- **NIS2** — Meridian Rail's participation in shared national rail infrastructure (the National Rail Interop Network) puts it in scope as critical infrastructure, with obligations the current estate has never been assessed against.

Unlike the other drivers, this one carries real compliance deadlines and potential penalties, not just competitive or operational pressure. These requirements come with concrete deadlines, and span beyond the technical capabilities addressed in the platform modernization goal.
