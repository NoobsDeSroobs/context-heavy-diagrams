# Goal: Grow Ridership

Reverse the multi-year decline in passenger volumes. While the platform modernization work removes a real reason people choose alternatives, increasing ridership also requires changes to pricing, scheduling, and mitigating efforts against factors well outside this program's scope.

Included on this diagram because it's the goal the task force is ultimately accountable to the business for. The other two goals are means, this one (alongside compliance) is closer to an end in itself.
