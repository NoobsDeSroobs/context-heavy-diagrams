# Driver: Rising Customer Expectations

Passengers now routinely compare Meridian Rail's booking experience against airlines, ride-sharing apps, and other rail operators who've already modernized. A booking flow that felt acceptable five years ago now reads as dated by comparison, despite improvements we have made to our systems.

A passenger who finds the experience frustrating has more alternatives to switch to than they used to.

Highlights opportunities: it's a direct reason to modernize the platform itself
