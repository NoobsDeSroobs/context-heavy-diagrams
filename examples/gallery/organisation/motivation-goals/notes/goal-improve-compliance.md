# Goal: Improve Regulatory Compliance

Bring Meridian Rail's data handling and critical-infrastructure posture up to what GDPR and NIS2 actually require, rather than continuing to operate on assumptions about compliance that were never formally assessed.

Deliberately kept as its own goal, separate from platform modernization, even though the two overlap in practice. The reasoning: compliance has its own deadlines and its own accountable owner, and folding it entirely into the platform goal risks it being treated as a nice-to-have feature of the rebuild rather than an obligation that should be tracked independently.
