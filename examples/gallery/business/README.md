# Business

Business-facing diagrams, for whoever produces or reads them: business architects, product owners and others.

- **`customer-journey.chd`**: customer journey, booking a ticket. Nine steps (realise the need, search, compare, select, enter details, pay, confirm, travel, post-trip loyalty credit) as a single left-to-right sequence, with no containers and no links, the simplest diagram in the gallery. Every step has a note tying it back to the systems in the [technical](../technical/README.md) diagrams.
