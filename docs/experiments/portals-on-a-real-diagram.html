Portals on a real diagram

The Meridian Rail landscape of the gallery (81 elements, 83 edges), a window of it where edges cross one another and run across elements, drawn with straight, step and curved edges (the edges are approximations of the app's, not the app). A portal is a short gap cut where an edge would cross something, with a mark at both ends and the same number on both. This is a mock-up (epic 0205): nothing of it is in the app.

The alternative: line hops

The same straight edges with a small hop where an edge crosses one drawn before it. The crossings stay, so a busy stretch gets no quieter; it only tells which line goes on. This is the sketch the epic asks for, not a candidate.

Setting a gap

One edge with two crossings and an element on its way. Two sliders set where the gap starts and ends, measured from the source; they are clamped so they never swap and never touch the elements. Add portals puts a short gap at the first crossing. On the canvas there would be a drag handle for each end too (not mocked).

Edge: Source to Target

A portal point

One reading of the idea: three edges run into one point and three come out of the matching point, so one carried link stands for many edges. The two points share the number. What the point holds (a list of edges, or just a name), and whether the edges are real edges of the diagram or something else, is open.
