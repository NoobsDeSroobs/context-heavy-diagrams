Portals on a real diagram

The Meridian Rail landscape of the gallery (81 elements, 83 edges), a window of it where edges cross one another and run across elements. The edges are drawn straight between their connection points here, not as the app draws them. Each portal is a short gap cut where an edge would cross something, with a mark at both ends and the same number on both. This is a mock-up (epic 0205): nothing of it is in the app.
