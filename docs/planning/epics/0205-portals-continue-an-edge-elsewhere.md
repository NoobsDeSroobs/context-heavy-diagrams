# 0205: Portals: continue an edge somewhere else

## Status
Built (2026-11-27) on branch `epic/0205-portals`; the maintainer's try-out in the app is what is left. Logged 2026-11-21 with the maintainer; mock-up done and the main choices made 2026-11-26.

## Goal
A complex diagram becomes criss-crossed. A **portal** lets an edge go in at one place and come out at another, so it does not have to run across the diagram and over everything else.

## The idea
- **A portalled edge is still one ordinary edge:** one source, one target, its tags, style, visibility sets and review behaviour, so the two-ended edge model everything is built on stays. It is drawn broken: a short stub leaves the source with a mark and a name, and a matching stub arrives at the target.
- **Leaning (2026-11-25): a portal is an optional property of any edge, not a routing style and not a type.** The edge keeps its routing (straight, step, smooth step, curved), its legend line, markers and animation; only the middle of its route is left undrawn. This replaces the earlier leaning of `routingStyle: "portal"` (2026-11-21), which would have cost an edge its own shape. Stored as an additive field on the edge, such as `portal: { start, end }`, the two places along the path where the visible edge stops and starts again, in pixels from the source; absent means a whole edge. draw.io maps it to a whole edge. `route_edges` and the edge operations of the assistant tools must know the field.
- **An edge has one portal or none** (decided 2026-11-26; a list can come later without breaking a file that holds one); jumping over many edges is a bigger gap, set by the person. **The two portals sit close together, where the edge would cross something.** A portal is for skipping a crossing, or an element in the way, when there is no other way: the hidden part is short, and most of the edge stays visible. The mark before the obstacle and the mark after it are the two ends. It is a line jump in the sense that the line skips the crossing, but the line goes in at one mark and comes out at the other, and the numbers say which belong together. (An earlier reading here, with short stubs next to the elements and the edge hidden between them, was wrong.) The nearer the two marks are, the easier the diagram is to read.
- **Setting them:** the two ends are two positions along the path, a slider for each in the edge's panel (as for the label position) and a drag handle for each on the canvas. **Add portals** (decided 2026-11-26) puts a gap of a fixed size in the middle of the edge; the person moves the two ends to where it belongs. Finding the crossings for them is not in the first version. The ends are clamped so they never swap or touch the elements, and an edge too short for a gap is drawn whole.
- **Matching the two ends: a number, never colour; both marks, by preference** (decided 2026-11-26; the mark is a per-person preference and is never stored in the diagram). At both ends a mark and a number, all portals in one colour. The mark is a tilde (a curved S turned across the edge, like the break in a line) or an oval (the entry oval drawn over the edge so the line goes into it, the exit oval under it so the line comes out of it, which shows the direction); each person picks one in Preferences. The oval's fill and ring colours would be settable (the fill may follow the canvas background). Whatever the mark, the two portals are best kept close to each other. Hovering a badge highlights the other end and the whole edge, and a click on it goes to the other end. How the number is made (from the order in the file, or a name the person types) is open.
- **Ctrl hover and selection** on a portalled edge always show the whole edge, drawn over everything else.
- **Exports draw a portal as seen on the canvas wherever the format can** (decided 2026-11-26): the SVG and PNG exports show the broken edge with its marks and numbers. **draw.io** gets the whole edge, with all its clutter: it has no portals.
- **The gap:** 60 units by default, 30 on each side of the middle of the edge (decided 2026-11-26). When an element moves, the two positions stay as they are, measured from the source, until the person changes or removes the portal; an edge that becomes too short for its gap is drawn whole.
- **The number:** automatic, from the order of the portalled edges in the file, and the person can type another. A diagnostics check reports duplicate numbers (decided 2026-11-26).
- **An edge label** that falls in the hidden middle is left to the person, at most a diagnostics warning (decided 2026-11-26).
- **A portal is only a way of drawing** (decided 2026-11-26). Behind the scenes a portalled edge is a whole edge: the layout check, crossings, compare, search, review and the legend all see it whole. The legend carries the edge's style, which means something; a portal does not.
- **The layout check stays as it is** (decided 2026-11-26): it sees a portalled edge whole and reports its crossings as before. There is no score for portals; a portal is only a less bad outcome than a crossing. The assistant is told so in its guidance: a crossing on an edge that has a portal is a lower priority than other layout problems, and a portal is a valid solution when no better one is found, after routing and moving have been tried. The assistant tools may set a portal on that basis, and keep one when they edit or route an edge.
- **Animation:** an animated edge runs along the edge as one thing and the hidden part is not drawn (decided 2026-11-26). A pulse that jumps across the gap smoothly and at once is real work and not in the first version.

## Not doing
- **Portal points** (a place that carries a bunch of connections to another place, teleporting many edges at once): not now. If a use case turns up, it gets its own design round. The mock-up has one reading of it.
- **Stacked layers of edges:** showing and hiding visibility sets already is that.
- **Line hops** (a small hop where two lines cross): can be sketched as a complement, but the maintainer rarely likes the result.

## Open questions
- Format: an additive field on the edge (positions from the source, an optional typed number), with `docs/file-format.md` changing with it.
- The relay bus of [0065](later/0065-bus-relay-node.md) is a different idea and stays parked; it may turn out to need nothing once portals exist.
